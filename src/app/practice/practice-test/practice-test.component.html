KCET Practice Test

Time: 2 hours

This practice test simulates the experience you will have taking the KCET on test day.

When you finish the test, we will give you a score estimate which will help you predict how well you would do on the actual KCET. You'll also be able to review your answers and see the video explanations.

Preparation:

  • Use a pen or pencil and scratch paper to write notes and work out problems.
  • An on-screen calculator is provided for the sections where one is allowed.
  • Set aside about 2 hours for a full test. Let your family and friends know not to interrupt you during this time. :)
We recommend taking the entire test in one sitting to practice your pacing and build your test-taking stamina. Good luck!
